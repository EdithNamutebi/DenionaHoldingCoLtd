Our Strategy

Balanced for Stability. Positioned for Growth.

We don't chase every opportunity — we balance the portfolio deliberately. The majority of our businesses generate steady cash flow that funds the group, while a focused share goes to high-growth bets that build our future.

01

Real Estate & Property

The classic holdings play — cash flow + appreciation.
Starting Point

Residential rentals, short-term rentals (Airbnb), small commercial units.

Growth Path

Land banking, warehousing, and a property management arm.

Why it works: Tangible asset, inflation hedge, and debt can be leveraged safely.
02

Finance & Investments

Hold and grow capital.
Starting Point

Treasury bonds, money market funds, private lending to SMEs.

Growth Path

Microfinance, fintech partnerships, private equity stakes in SMEs.

Why it works: Keeps cash working and builds relationships for deal flow.
03

Logistics & Supply Chain

Movement of goods = constant demand.
Starting Point

3PL warehousing, last-mile delivery fleet, clearing & forwarding.

Growth Path

Cold chain logistics and cross-border haulage.

Why it works: High cash velocity and easy to scale regionally.
04

Technology & Digital Services

Low capex, high margin.
Starting Point

SaaS reselling, IT services for SMEs, web & app development agency.

Growth Path

Our own SaaS product, data services, and fintech.

Why it works: Scales fast with minimal physical assets.
05

Agriculture & Agro-processing

Africa's backbone sector.
Starting Point

Contract farming, produce trading, small processing unit.

Growth Path

Own farms, an export arm, and branded FMCG products.

Why it works: Essential demand, government incentives, and export potential.
06

Energy & Infrastructure

High barrier, high reward.
Starting Point

Solar installations for homes & SMEs, generator sales and service.

Growth Path

Mini-grids, EPC contracting, and IPP projects.

Why it works: Everyone needs power — and it's recurring revenue.
07

Healthcare & Wellness

Non-cyclical demand.
Starting Point

Pharmacy chain, medical equipment supply, clinics.

Growth Path

Diagnostics lab and health insurance brokerage.

Why it works: Stable cash flow and high trust when done right.
08

Education & Training

Recession-resistant.
Starting Point

Skills training center, e-learning platform, educational supplies.

Growth Path

A private school and vocational institute.

Why it works: High demand, low churn — and a strong social impact story for investors.

Hospitality & Leisure

Cash-heavy, brand-driven.
Starting Point

Restaurant/café, events space, travel agency.

Growth Path

Hotels, resorts, and tourism services.

Why it works: Brand visibility — the public face of the Deniona name.

Manufacturing & Trading

Make or move products.

Import/export trading and FMCG distribution.

Growth Path

Light manufacturing, packaging, and branded products.
