Strategic Objectives

Five Commitments. Every One Measurable.

These objectives guide every business unit in the Deniona portfolio — and we hold ourselves publicly accountable to them.

01

Build a Resilient, Multi-Sector Portfolio

Grow Deniona Holdings into a diversified Ugandan company with strong revenue streams from logistics, supplies, and real estate — while using profits to fund high-impact social programs.

02

Deliver Reliable, Local Solutions

Become the go-to partner for SMEs and communities needing dependable logistics, quality supplies, and accessible services.

How we measure it: 95% on-time delivery  •  80%+ client retention across all business units.
03

Create Jobs & Local Value

Prioritize hiring, training, and sourcing locally — so value keeps circulating in the communities we serve, not leaking out of them.

How we measure it: 150+ direct jobs  •  500+ indirect income opportunities for Ugandans by 2028.
04

Drive Impact Through Innovation & Partnerships

Use technology — WhatsApp, SMS, and digital kits — to scale reach at low cost. Partner with faith leaders, clinics, schools, and development agencies to make programs culturally accepted and donor-ready.

05

Operate With Integrity & Sustainability

Maintain transparent governance, ethical practices, and financial discipline across all entities.

How we measure it: 10% of business unit profits reinvested annually into community projects.
